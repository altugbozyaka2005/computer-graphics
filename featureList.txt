General: C++17, standard library only; builds with make (source/Makefile) to output/CGR26_renderer with no warnings
General: pbrt-v4 scene parser (tokenizer, parameter lists, graphics state, Include/Import, ObjectBegin/ObjectInstance, named materials, coordinate systems)
General: integrator chosen by the scene file: Integrator "whitted" (WSRT) or "distributed" (DRT); pbrt "path"/"volpath" mapped to distributed with a warning
General: .log file written next to every output image, with errors and warnings as file:line:column messages
General: graceful handling of syntax errors and unsupported features: recoverable problems fall back to defaults, fatal ones are logged without a crash
General: profiling in the log: parse/load, render, tone-map/write times; camera/secondary/shadow ray counts, ray-primitive tests, Mrays/s, and the number of rays traced at each depth of the ray tree
General: multithreaded tile renderer (std::thread) with deterministic per-pixel random streams (PCG32)
General: output as 8-bit PNG (own encoder) and linear HDR PFM, plus optional PPM
Camera: perspective camera matching pbrt-v4 conventions (fov on the shorter image axis, LookAt, screenwindow, frameaspectratio)
Camera (Module 2): orthographic camera (pbrt-v4 "orthographic": parallel rays from the z = 0 plane, extent set by the screen window in camera-space units, optional thin lens as in pbrt-v4); framing checked against pbrt-v4
WSRT: ideal specular reflection, recursive up to maxdepth
WSRT: adaptive ray-tree depth control: reflection/refraction branches whose accumulated weight (Fresnel x Kr x Kt) falls below 0.001 are not traced
WSRT: specular refraction with Snell's law, exact dielectric Fresnel equations and total internal reflection
WSRT: Blinn-Phong shading (ambient, diffuse, specular) with Kd (or Kd texture), Ks, Ka, shininess per material
WSRT: point lights with configurable position and radiant intensity (also spot and distant lights)
WSRT: hard shadows with shadow rays and a normal-offset ray origin against self-shadowing
WSRT and DRT: optional filtered (coloured, transparent) shadows: shadow rays through dielectrics are attenuated by Fresnel transmission and Kt (Integrator "bool transparentshadows"); no caustics. In the DRT, refracted paths reaching a light after a diffuse bounce are then not counted again
DRT: optional adaptive path termination ("float minweight": stop a path whose throughput falls below the threshold) and "bool caustics" false (ignore light reached via mirror/glass after a diffuse bounce)
Lights: area lights with "integer samples" 0 are visible-only emitters (seen directly and in mirrors/glass, no illumination)
Geometry: alpha cut-out masks on any shape (pbrt "float alpha" / "texture alpha"), honoured by camera and shadow rays
WSRT: UV-mapped textures for diffuse colour from PPM files (bilinear filtering, repeat/clamp/black wrap), plus procedural checkerboard, scale and mix textures
WSRT: geometry: planes (infinite, extension), spheres (including partial), triangles, disks, with ray intersection and UV generation for each
WSRT: area lights approximated as point lights at their centroid (hard shadows)
DRT: triangle meshes from OBJ (v/vt/vn, polygons, negative indices) and PLY (ascii, binary) files and inline trianglemesh/bilinearmesh, with per-vertex normals (smooth shading) and UVs
DRT: textures on meshes through interpolated UVs
DRT: area lights (emissive triangles/quads, disks, spheres; one- or two-sided) with soft shadows from shadow rays to points on the light; number of shadow rays configurable (lightsamples, or per-light samples)
Sampler comparison (Module 2): area-light sample patterns (Integrator "string lightsampling", --lightsampling): uniform (independent random points), grid (n x n cell centres, deterministic), halton (first N points of the 2-D Halton sequence, bases 2 and 3, Cranley-Patterson rotated per shading point; "string haltonrandomization" "none" turns the rotation off), and stratified (one random point per n x n cell, an extra comparator); grid and stratified round N to n x n (logged)
Sampler comparison (Module 2): an emissive rectangle (two triangles) is detected and sampled through its exact (u, v) parametrisation, so grid cells are true cells of the rectangle
DRT: energy-conserving modified Phong BRDF (kd/pi + ks (n+2)/(2pi) cos^n); kd clamped so that kd+ks+kr <= 1
DRT: indirect light with uniform-hemisphere rays up to maxdepth; emission skipped after diffuse bounces (no double counting)
DRT (Module 2): branching factor BF (Integrator "integer branchingfactor", --bf): BF rays at every diffuse/glossy scattering event, so a camera ray spawns up to BF^d rays at depth d; maxdepth is MD. Delta lobes are not multiplied (a mirror casts one ray); with BF >= 2 a dielectric traces both reflection and refraction weighted by Fresnel (splitting) instead of choosing one
DRT (Module 2): rays of the last level (depth = MD) that could only return environment light are not traced when the scene has no environment (identical images, fewer rays)
DRT: dielectrics and mirrors (Fresnel-weighted stochastic reflect/refract choice)
DRT: thin-lens depth of field (lensradius, focaldistance) with rays jittered over the aperture
DRT: Reinhard tone mapping on luminance (default) or per channel, configurable white point, optional key and exposure
Antialiasing (Module 2): supersampling, the samples of a pixel averaged with equal weights: Sampler "independent" (uniform random; 1 sample = pixel centre), "stratified" (xsamples x ysamples cells; jitter true: jittered, false: regular grid at the cell centres; lens samples stratified and shuffled), "halton" (bases 2, 3 on the film and 5, 7 on the lens, Cranley-Patterson rotated per pixel; randomization "none" = the same points in every pixel)
Antialiasing (Module 2): PixelFilter "box" with configurable xradius/yradius (samples drawn from [-r, r]^2 around the pixel centre, as pbrt-v4's filter importance sampling)
Command line (Module 2): --sampler, --nojitter, --spp, --bf, --maxdepth, --lightsampling, --pixelbounds, --cropwindow overrides (each recorded in the log); Film "integer pixelbounds"
Lights: blackbody and spectral inputs converted to RGB with an analytic CIE 1931 fit (approximation; rendering itself is RGB)
Partial: rough conductors and dielectrics rendered as smooth; coateddiffuse and named metal spectra approximated
Partial: infinite lights support constant radiance only (no environment maps)
Partial: no BVH; one bounding box per object (slab test) lets a ray skip all primitives of an object it misses
Partial: Halton pixel sampler randomization approximated by a Cranley-Patterson rotation (pbrt-v4 permutes digits); zsobol, sobol, paddedsobol and pmj02bn samplers are mapped to halton (warning)
Not implemented: reconstruction filters other than box (gaussian, triangle, mitchell, sinc fall back to box with a warning); adaptive sampling (only discussed as an alternative)
Not implemented: participating media, displacement and normal mapping, PNG/EXR texture input
Validation: scenes/validation (analytic tests, pbrt-v4 reference comparisons, 18 broken scenes) checked by scenes/tools/validate_module1.py into output/validation.log
Validation (Module 2): scenes/module2/validation checked by scenes/tools/validate_module2.py into output/module2/validation.log: orthographic camera vs pbrt-v4, orthographic size invariance, field of view vs exact projection, supersampling vs exact pixel coverage, light-sample patterns vs an analytic form factor, BF unbiasedness and noise / sqrt(BF), dielectric splitting, BF^d rays per depth, thread determinism
Studies (Module 2): scenes/tools/module2_study.py renders every Module 2 image and writes the measurements (output/module2/*.csv, module2.log): camera sizes and wide-angle stretching, antialiasing error and render time vs spp, sampler RMSE vs a pbrt-v4 reference over 24 seeds, BF/MD sweeps with energy, noise, error and equal-time comparisons; scenes/tools/make_figures_m2.py draws the annotated figures and plots
